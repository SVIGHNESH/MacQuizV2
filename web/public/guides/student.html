Walkthrough № MQ-S1 For the candidate

Sit the paper. Nothing else to manage.

MacQuiz is built so that the only thing you have to think about during a test is the question in front of you. Your answers save themselves, the clock is not yours to argue with, and a dead battery is not the end of your paper. Here is the whole thing, start to finish.

Section A - Before the paper

Getting in, and finding what is coming.

You do not create your own account. Your college does, and hands you the details.

  1. Q1Sign in

    Sign in and change the temporary password

    Use the email and temporary password you were given. The very first time, MacQuiz will stop you on a password screen before letting you go anywhere - pick your own password there. There is no sign-up page and no way to make yourself an account.

  2. Q2Your list

    See only the papers meant for you

    Your dashboard shows the quizzes you have actually been assigned - nothing else on the platform is visible to you, even if you know its address. Each one shows its subject, when it opens, and how long you will get.

    A paper that has not opened yet shows a countdown. You cannot start early, and you do not need to keep the page open waiting - it opens on its own.

  3. Q3Optional

    Make your profile yours

    Pick one of the built-in stickers or upload a photo, and change your password whenever you like, from the profile panel behind your name. Purely cosmetic - it changes nothing about how a paper works.

Section B - During the paper

Once you start, the clock is running.

Two limits apply at once, and it is worth understanding which one will reach you first.

  1. Q4Start

    Start when you are ready - but not too late

    Your personal timer begins when you press start, not when the window opened. But the paper also has a hard end time for everyone.

    Which limit hits you You get your full duration or the time left until the paper closes - whichever is shorter. Start a 60-minute paper 20 minutes before it closes and you have 20 minutes, not 60. Starting promptly is the whole trick.
  2. Q5Answer

    Answer in any order; everything saves itself

    Move freely between questions and change your mind as often as you like. Every answer is saved a couple of seconds after you touch it - there is no save button to forget, and no moment where your work only exists in the browser.

    The countdown on screen is a convenience. The real deadline is held by the server, so changing your device's clock does exactly nothing.

  3. Q6Rules

    Stay in the window

    Your teacher may switch on some or all of these for a paper. You will be told which are active when you start.

    Fullscreen

    Lock

    The paper takes over the screen. Leaving fullscreen is noticed, and you are asked to return before carrying on.

    Tab switching

    Tracked

    Switching tabs or windows is recorded, with how long you were away.

    Copy & paste

    Blocked

    Copy, paste and right-click are disabled inside the paper.

    One device

    Always

    Opening the same paper on a second device ends the session on the first. Always on.

    If something is counted against you, a small warning appears - "violation 2 of 3", so you always know where you stand. Reaching the limit usually just marks your row for the teacher to look at; on some papers it ends the attempt. Either way your teacher sees what happened and decides. Nothing accuses you automatically.

Section C - If something goes wrong

The honest answers to "what if…"

These are the four things students actually worry about. All four have a defined outcome.

My WiFi dropped

Recoverable

Everything you had answered is already saved on the server. Get back online, reopen the paper, and you resume exactly where you were with the correct time remaining. You lose the minutes you were offline, not the answers.

My laptop died

Recoverable

Same answer, and one more safety net: if you never make it back, the server submits your saved answers for you when your time runs out. An unfinished paper is still marked on what you had done.

I ran out of time

Handled

Nothing is lost and nothing is dramatic. At the deadline your attempt submits itself with everything saved. You do not need to be watching the clock or present at all.

I was removed from the paper

Ask your teacher

A teacher can end an attempt, and must record a reason - which is shown to you on screen. Your work is still marked, flagged as removed. If it was a misunderstanding, your teacher can re-admit you for a fresh attempt with the time that is left.

Worth knowing You do not need to submit for your work to count, and you cannot lose a paper by closing the wrong tab. The only thing that genuinely costs you is starting late, because that comes straight out of your own time.
Section D - After the paper

Where your marks turn up.

Marking begins as soon as the paper closes. When you get to see it is your teacher's call.

  1. Q7Result

    Your score, once it is released

    Some papers release automatically the moment marking finishes; others wait until your teacher releases them by hand. Until that happens the result simply is not there yet - it is not an error, and refreshing will not hurry it.

  2. Q8Review

    Go back through the paper

    Once released you can walk the whole paper again: what you answered, what was correct, and the marks for each question. You also get a percentile, so you know roughly where you sat in the class rather than just your raw score.

  3. Q9Over time

    Watch your own trend

    Across papers you can see your accuracy trend, your average time per question, and - when your teacher has tagged the questions by topic - which topics you are strong and weak in.

    Only questions you actually answered count towards a topic, so skipping a question is never recorded as a weakness in that topic.

Before your next paper

Four things to do, in order.

1 · Check the window
Know when it opens and closes, and how long you get. Starting late costs you your own time.
2 · Sit somewhere stable
Reliable power and network. Both are survivable, but neither is fun mid-paper.
3 · Close everything else
If tab tracking is on for the paper, an innocent switch still gets recorded.
4 · Answer, do not manage
No saving, no submitting in a panic at the end. Just answer until the clock stops.
